# Mantel Family Messages: Terms and Conditions

Last updated: November 22, 2026

**Program name:** Mantel Family Messages
**Operator:** Matt Elhart (private household use)

1. **Description.** Mantel Family Messages sends personal notes, reminders, and drawings from a private household calendar display to members of the operator's immediate family who have opted in. It is not a commercial or marketing service.
2. **Opt-in.** You receive messages only after you text START or YES to the program number. See the [opt-in page](opt-in.md).
3. **Frequency.** Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per week.
4. **Cost.** Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.
5. **Opt-out.** Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one final confirmation message and no further texts.
6. **Help.** Reply HELP for help, or contact the operator directly.
7. **Carriers.** Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
8. **Privacy.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
