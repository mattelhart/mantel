# Mantel Family Messages: How Recipients Opt In

Mantel is a private, self-hosted family wall calendar run by Matt Elhart for his own household. Its "Family Messages" feature sends short notes and hand-drawn doodles from the kitchen wall display to the phones of immediate family members. It is not used for marketing, promotions, or any third party.

## Who receives messages

Only members of the Elhart household whose phone numbers are entered by the account owner in Mantel's Settings → Family screen. No numbers are purchased, collected from the public, or shared.

## How consent is collected

1. The account owner asks the family member in person whether they want to receive texts from the household calendar number.
2. Before any message is sent, the family member texts **START** (or **YES**) from their own phone to the Mantel toll-free number.
3. Mantel replies with this confirmation of enrollment:

   > Mantel Family: You're signed up for family notes from the household calendar. Msg frequency varies, usually a few per week. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

4. Messages are only sent to numbers that have completed step 2.

## Opting out

Replying **STOP** at any time immediately stops all messages. Replying **HELP** returns: "Mantel Family: household calendar messages from Matt Elhart. Reply STOP to opt out."

## Sample messages

- "Mantel: Dinner's ready!"
- "Mantel: Soccer practice moved to 5:30 today."
- "Mantel: (picture) Mom sent you a doodle."
