# Mantel Family Messages: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Mantel is a private, self-hosted family calendar operated by Matt Elhart for his own household.

- **What we collect.** The mobile phone numbers of opted-in family members, and the content of messages sent to or from the household calendar.
- **How it is used.** Only to deliver and display family messages on the household calendar and the recipients' phones.
- **Where it is stored.** On a private server in the operator's home. Messages are transmitted through Twilio, our SMS provider.
- **Sharing.** We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or messages with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.
- **Opt-out and deletion.** Reply STOP to stop messages. Ask the operator to delete your number and message history at any time.
